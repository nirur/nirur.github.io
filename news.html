This is a news aggregator. The aggregator is hungry for RSS feeds, so let me know if you encounter any.

Below are the SCCLD login pages for various news sites; you may find them useful.

  • NY times. . There's a separate link for historic issues; this is via the ProQuest database. (This lets you make RSS feeds, but for historic issues I'm not sure RSS is very useful.)
  • Wall Street Journal. SCCLD also offers a ProQuest version, which lets you make RSS feeds out of custom queries. Not sure how quickly the database updates with breaking news, though. This might also get you access to Market Watch, you should try it.
  • Scientific American.
  • EBSCO. This is a database which gives you (slightly outdated) access to Bloomberg (.com and Newsweek), Forbes, and the Atlantic. The site is really finicky though (you need to fiddle around to find the right databases; not all of the available ones are listed at the top). Also, I haven't yet added The Atlantic to this feed.
