This is a news aggregator. The aggregator is hungry for RSS feeds, so let me know if you encounter any.

Here's SCCLD's resource page, which tends to be highly useful. Below are a select few:

  • New York Times. There's a separate link for historic issues; this is via the ProQuest database. (This lets you make RSS feeds, but for historic issues I'm not sure RSS is very useful.)
  • Wall Street Journal. SCCLD also offers a ProQuest version, which lets you make RSS feeds out of custom queries. Not sure how quickly the database updates with breaking news, though.
  • Scientific American.
  • EBSCO. This is a database which gives you (slightly outdated) access to Bloomberg (.com and Newsweek), Forbes, and the Atlantic (and much more.) The site is really finicky though (you need to fiddle around to find the right databases; not all of the available ones are listed at the top; sometimes the website isn't working, and you need to come back a few days later).
  • Science.org. This is a publication by the AAAS. I didn't include it here because the RSS feeds tend to be poorly curated, but the website is perfectly good.
